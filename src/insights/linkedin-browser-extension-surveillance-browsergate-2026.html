BrowserGate: LinkedIn Illegally Searching Your Browser Extensions

Source: Hacker News | Score: 4.5/5 | Date: 2026-04-03

🚨 Major Privacy Scandal: Fairlinked e.V. reveals LinkedIn is running one of the largest corporate espionage operations in modern history - scanning users' computers for installed software, browser extensions, and competitor tools.

What They Found

Mass Breach of Personal Data

LinkedIn's scan reveals:

  • Religious beliefs and political opinions
  • Disabilities and health conditions
  • Job search activity (including 509 job search tools)
  • Practicing Muslims (via religious extensions)
  • Political orientation (via political extensions)
  • Neurodivergent users (via specialized extensions)

Under EU law, this data is not regulated - it's prohibited. LinkedIn has no consent, no disclosure, and no legal basis.

Corporate Espionage & Trade Secret Theft

  • Scans for 200+ products that compete with LinkedIn's sales tools
  • Targets Apollo, Lusha, ZoomInfo, and hundreds more
  • Maps which companies use which competitor products
  • Extracts customer lists from users' browsers without consent
  • Has sent enforcement threats to users of third-party tools using data from this covert scanning

Deceiving EU Regulators

In 2023, the EU designated LinkedIn as a regulated gatekeeper under the Digital Markets Act and ordered it to open its platform.

  • LinkedIn published two restricted APIs handling ~0.07 calls per second
  • Meanwhile, LinkedIn operates internal API "Voyager" at 163,000 calls per second
  • In Microsoft's 249-page compliance report, "API" appears 533 times, "Voyager" 0 times
  • Surveillance list grew from ~461 products in 2024 to 6,000+ by February 2026

Data Shared with Third Parties

  • Loads invisible tracking element from HUMAN Security (formerly PerimeterX)
  • Zero-pixel wide tracking hidden off-screen
  • Sets cookies without user knowledge
  • Fingerprinting script from LinkedIn's own servers
  • Third script from Google executes silently on every page load
  • All encrypted and undisclosed

Legal Implications

This is illegal and potentially a criminal offense in every jurisdiction examined. The investigation is being conducted by Fairlinked e.V., an association of commercial LinkedIn users.

Why It Matters

  • Scale: One billion LinkedIn users affected worldwide
  • Privacy: Mass surveillance without consent or disclosure
  • Competition: Using surveillance data to target competitor users
  • Regulation: Demonstrates systematic deception of EU regulators
  • Security: Data shared with third parties including foreign cybersecurity firms
