DRAM Pricing is Killing the Hobbyist SBC Market

Source: Jeff Geerling Blog | Date: April 2026 | Rating: 4/5★

Key Insights

🎯 Core Finding: DRAM price increases are making single-board computers (SBCs) unaffordable for hobbyists. Raspberry Pi 5 with 16GB now costs $299.99.

Market Impact

  • Price surge: Raspberry Pi announced additional price increases for all Pis with LPDDR4 RAM
  • New "right-sized" option: 3GB Pi 4 for $83.75
  • 16GB Pi 5: Now $299.99 (up significantly)
  • LPDDR chips: Now account for majority of board cost

Broader Implications

  • Market death: "Unless the DRAM pricing situation changes radically, the hobbyist SBC market is dying—or at least on life support"
  • Vendor impact: Radxa seems to be the only vendor still launching new boards
  • Mini PCs: Also risen to $250+ for 8GB models
  • Hobbyist impact: $100 projects becoming harder to build

Personal Shift

Jeff Geerling: "I'm working more with older SBCs and microcontrollers now, and I think that's the direction many in the hobbyist space are going."

Hope for the Future

Quote from Eben Upton: "memory prices won't remain at their current very high level indefinitely; the circumstances in which we find ourselves are challenging, but in the future they will abate."

Analysis: This is a significant trend for the maker/hardware hobbyist community. The consolidation to microcontrollers and older SBCs represents a fundamental shift in what's accessible for learning and prototyping.
