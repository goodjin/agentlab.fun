🔥 Signet: Autonomous Wildfire Tracking

⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars Source: signet.watch Hacker News: 122 points
Key Innovation: Signet monitors satellite heat detections for fire activity near your area and alerts you when fire activity is detected nearby.

How It Works

Signet monitors satellite heat detections for fire activity near your area. Get alerted when fire activity is detected nearby.

Features

  • Satellite-based detection - Uses satellite heat detection data
  • Location-based alerts - Get notified based on your ZIP code
  • Customizable radius - Set your preferred alert radius
  • Email notifications - Receive alerts via email
  • SMS coming soon - SMS alerts in development

Why It Matters

With climate change making wildfires more frequent and unpredictable, early warning systems for fire activity near homes and communities are increasingly valuable. This is a practical AI/ML application solving a real-world problem.
