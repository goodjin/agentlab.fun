Steve Yegge Wants You to Stop Looking at Your Code

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5星 来源: O'Reilly Radar
来源: O'Reilly Radar
作者: Tim O'Reilly
日期: 2026年3月12日

核心内容

Tim O'Reilly 与 Steve Yegge 的深度对话，关于 AI 编程代理如何改变软件开发行业。

关键亮点

1. 八级程序员进化框架

  • Level 1-4: 越来越复杂的 IDE 使用
  • Level 5-8: 编程代理时代
  • 关键转折点: Level 5 - 你的 IDE 消失，永远不再打开
  • 一旦你意识到 Claude Code 可以写代码，你就用它像乐高一样组装

2. 每个人都有首席助理

"We all have a chief of staff now. Everybody's going to be able to spend their time more productively on whatever they want to spend it on instead of figuring out where the printer is jammed."

Steve 将 Gas Town 类比为亚马逊高管的执行助理 - 每个人现在都是"高管"，有 AI 助手处理琐事。

3. AI 吸血鬼 - 新型倦怠

  • 旧版过度工作：公司给你堆任务直到崩溃
  • 新版：AI 说"Is there anything else you'd like me to do?" 你说是是是
  • 问题：AI 解决所有简单问题，留给你的全是硬骨头
  • Steve 现在每天小睡两次，感到认知强度带来的疲惫

4. 苦 Lesson 的正确理解

实用测试： 如果你写的代码是想让 AI 更聪明（添加启发式、解析器、正则表达式），你就在苦 lesson 的错误一边。

Steve 每天至少五次用苦 lesson 作为数学公式来思考。

5. 代码是液体

"If you're looking at your code, then you're in a Formula One race and you've parked your car and opened the hood and you're looking at the engine. You've slowed time to the point where everyone is racing past you and you're a frozen statue. Code is a liquid. You spray it through hoses."

6. 品味是护城河

  • "Everything is going to come down to taste"
  • 公司不再有优势 - 有好点子的创业者可以用开源模型构建重要产品
  • 这是个人创造巨大影响的黄金机会

7. 悲伤的五个阶段

Steve 认为整个开发者社区正处于"否认"阶段：

"What we're doing is we're at the end of an era. An age, a golden age, maybe, where we programmers, we're writing all the code. And it was wonderful for 30, 40, 50 years or whatever. That era is ending and people are grieving because of it."

8. 我们会建造更大的东西

面对 AI 削弱批判性思维的担忧，Steve 的回答是：

"We're going to build bigger stuff. That's what everyone's worried about. What's going to happen? And the answer is we're going to build bigger stuff and it's going to be fun."
