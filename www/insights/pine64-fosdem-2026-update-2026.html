Pine64 FOSDEM 2026 Update

Source: pine64.org • Date: 2026-03-24 • Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
Linux Phone Pine64 FOSDEM Hardware Open Source

Overview

Pine64 community update from FOSDEM 2026, covering the state of Linux phones, PineNote, PineTab2, PineTime Pro, and the challenges facing the Linux phone ecosystem.

Key News: DRAM shortage halts PineTab2, PineNote, and PinePhone production. PinePhone 2 is "in limbo" due to financial risk and market shifts toward porting cheaper Android devices to Linux.

DRAM Shortage Impact

Current chip shortage affects:

  • PineTab 2 - production halted
  • PineNote - production halted
  • PinePhone - production halted

Unaffected: PineTime, PineBuds, Pinecil, PineVoice

PineTime Pro

New features:

  • AMOLED display
  • GPS
  • Custom chip
  • Digital crown with extra button
  • Blood oxygen sensor
  • Per-component power cutting for better battery life

Discussion ongoing about fundraising approach (device markup vs. service fee).

Where is the PinePhone 2?

Multiple factors keeping PinePhone 2 in limbo:

  • Financial riskiness - PineStore determined it's too high
  • DRAM shortage
  • Community interest shifted to porting cheaper Android devices to Sailfish and PostmarketOS

Potential future components discussed: RK3572/6 SOC, RedCap 5G modem (similar to Quectel EG25-G).

Community Edition Challenges

Creating community edition devices requires:

  • New PineStore SKU
  • Community agreeing to pay extra cost
  • Tax implications reducing developer compensation
  • Fund allocation complexity

Significance

This update reflects the challenges facing the Linux phone ecosystem. The shift toward porting Android devices rather than building dedicated Linux phones represents a significant trend in the mobile Linux space.
