LinkedIn Illegally Searching Your Computer

Source: Hacker News (1179 points) | BrowserGate Investigation

Key Findings

Critical Privacy Scandal: Microsoft/LinkedIn is running one of the largest corporate espionage operations in modern history - secretly scanning users' computers for installed software, competitors, and sensitive data.

What LinkedIn Is Doing

  • Mass surveillance: Hidden code searches users' computers for installed software on every visit to linkedin.com
  • Sensitive data collection: Detects religious beliefs, political opinions, disabilities, and job search activity
  • Corporate espionage: Scans for 200+ competitor products (Apollo, Lusha, ZoomInfo) to map which companies use which tools
  • Deceiving regulators: Used restricted APIs (0.07 calls/sec) to appear compliant while operating Voyager at 163,000 calls/sec
  • Third-party data sharing: Sends data to HUMAN Security (American-Israeli cybersecurity firm) and Google
Illegal: This violates EU GDPR, ePrivacy Directive, and potentially criminal statutes in multiple jurisdictions. No consent, no disclosure, no legal basis.

Scale

  • 1 billion LinkedIn users affected
  • Scan list grew from ~461 products (2024) to 6,000+ (February 2026)
  • LinkedIn knows each user's real name, employer, and job title

Why It Matters

This is a fundamental betrayal of user trust - a platform that professionals rely on for career networking is secretly fingerprinting their computers, tracking their job searches, and mapping competitor tool usage across companies.
